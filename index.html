How to play

Build: click roofs to grow up, walls to grow out. Alt-click to repaint. Right-click to remove.

Explore: middle-drag to orbit and tilt, Shift + middle-drag to pan, scroll to zoom. Q / E rotate; R recenters.

Connect: bridge two upper walls for an archway. Choose Balcony, then click an upper wall.

Shortcuts: 1–5 choose tools; B opens the menu; Escape closes it; Ctrl / Cmd + Z undoes.
