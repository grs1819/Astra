How to play

Camping: select Camp (7) and click dry ground for a lean-to. Click it again for a tent, then a glamping shelter. Adjacent camp tiles merge into one larger shelter. At 2 tiles a campfire appears on nearby empty dry ground; at 4 it gains chairs and a lantern. Role (8) assigns a purpose to existing buildings. Right-click removes; Undo restores.

Build: click roofs to grow up, walls to grow out. Alt-click to repaint. Right-click to remove.

Touch: drag one finger to orbit and tilt. Drag two fingers to pan; pinch to zoom. A quick tap builds; select Remove to erase. Camera gestures never place blocks.

Explore: middle-drag to orbit and tilt, Shift + middle-drag to pan, scroll to zoom. Q / E rotate; R recenters.

Roofing: choose a roof color. Cottages have natural moss over a colored roof; log cabins gain weathered ribbed metal; estates gain polished standing-seam panels. Two stories or three rooms create a log cabin; four stories or ten rooms create an estate.

Lookouts: stack at least three floors with a narrow one- or two-room summit to create panoramic windows, a telescope, map table, and woodburning stove. Add height to move the lookout upward.

Shape: connected cottages share sloped roofs. Build a taller neighbor to turn an exposed lower roof into a railed terrace. Raised entrances gain steps automatically.

Connect: bridge two upper walls for an archway. Choose Balcony, then click an upper wall.

Water: select Water (6), then Stream, River, or Lake. Click ground to add tiles; connected bodies gain detail as they grow. Cottages stand on stilts and paths become footbridges. Water can also be added beneath existing cottages and paths. With Water selected, right-click removes water only. Remove trees before adding water.

Village life: three cottages welcome residents. Connect at least three path tiles near a home to invite cyclists. Paths and footbridges give everyone safe routes around town.

Shortcuts: 1–6 choose tools; B opens the menu; Escape closes it; Ctrl / Cmd + Z undoes.
